# Visual grammar and source examples

These are independent presentation kits. The three photographs supplied by the user on 2 November 2026 are references for visual decisions, not game screenshots produced by this system. The actual kit assets are original vector illustrations.

## Bright Simulator — `bright-simulator`

Reference: [bright-simulator.png](images/bright-simulator.png).

Use a sunny, spacious playfield, clear colored landmarks and chunky silhouettes. Keep the main objective high and centered; place large illustrated navigation buttons on the left and resource counters near the lower left. White heavy labels with dark outlines and colored raised tiles should read against a bright world. Notifications attach to the relevant button. Shop cards focus on one large item picture with a direct action beneath it; the collection has an obvious selected state.

The world remains visible around the HUD. A restrained screen avoids the right-hand promotional rail and dense progression strip of Neon Clicker. Use studs as an optional material cue, not the only distinction between kits.

## Tactical Tycoon — `tactical-tycoon`

Reference: [tactical-tycoon.png](images/tactical-tycoon.png).

Use industrial silhouettes, a low horizon, metal details and contained cyan signals. Dark beveled panels, compact resource strips, explicit key hints and a bottom equipment belt frame the world. Navigation is a compact horizontal toolbar; menus and shops use side categories and an item list with a separate detail pane. Item illustrations remain essential even though the overall interface is quieter.

Use restrained amber for primary actions, cyan for placement/selection and red for cancellation. The kit should still be identifiable in grayscale by its horizontal information strips, list/detail arrangement and equipment slots.

## Neon Clicker — `neon-clicker`

Reference: [neon-clicker.png](images/neon-clicker.png).

Use a broad plaza, stepped display stands, strong portal silhouettes and luminous accents. The HUD has a two-column illustrated navigation rail, a compact right-hand event/boost rail, and a large bottom progress bar with action tiles. White outlined, slightly slanted labels and saturated gradients give strong hierarchy. Rebirth, gift, gear, world and potion use different symbols, not differently colored copies of one diamond.

Shop products are large illustrated promotion cards; inventory becomes a collection grid. A strong cyan/magenta energy treatment distinguishes this kit from the sunny Simulator. Preserve readable central play space on a phone by collapsing secondary tiles into a menu.

## Compare usefully

The differences concern navigation topology, world composition, information density, typography, item presentation and interaction hierarchy. Check the HUD, menu, shop and inventory rather than accepting a different palette as sufficient. Phone layouts rearrange groups and controls instead of shrinking the whole desktop interface.
